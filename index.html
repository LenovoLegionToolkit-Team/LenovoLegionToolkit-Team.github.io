Core Features

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Full hardware control for Lenovo laptops without intrusive telemetry, advertisements, or background daemons.

Power Modes & Limits

Instantly switch between Quiet, Balanced, Performance, and Custom power profiles. Tune CPU PL1/PL2, GPU TGP, and temperature limits with zero delay.

Battery Conservation

Preserve your laptop's battery lifespan by limiting charging to 80% (Conservation Mode) or toggle Rapid Charge when you're on the go.

Custom Fan Curves

Take complete control over your thermals with fully custom fan curve presets. Keep your system dead silent during work and cool under heavy gaming.

Display & GPU Modes

Switch GPU modes seamlessly (dGPU, Hybrid, Hybrid-iGPU, Auto), toggle screen refresh rates (60Hz / 165Hz / 240Hz), and enable Display Overdrive.

RGB & Dynamic Lighting

Manage Lenovo 4-Zone and Spectrum RGB backlighting, lightbars, and logo illumination, with direct integration for Windows Dynamic Lighting.

Automations & OSD

Automate profile changes when plugging into AC or running on battery, trigger actions on game launch, customize Fn keys, and enjoy clean OSD popups.
