Key Capabilities

Hardware Control Without Background Overhead

Direct communication with laptop Embedded Controller (EC), ACPI, and WMI interfaces.

Power Modes & Power Limits

Switch between Quiet, Balance, Performance, Extreme, and Custom power profiles. On supported models, Custom Mode enables configuration of CPU power limits (PL1/PL2/PL4), CPU temperature limits, and GPU TGP targets.

Battery Charging Controls

Toggle firmware Conservation Mode (60% threshold on 2021 and earlier models; 80% on 2022 and later models), Rapid Charge, Night Charge, and configurable charge start/stop thresholds on supported systems.

Custom Fan Curves

10-point fan speed curve customization in Custom Mode on Gen 7 and newer laptops whose firmware exposes fan table registers. Includes manual Full Fan Speed toggle.

Hybrid GPU & Display Modes

Control GPU operating states: dGPU Only, Hybrid, Hybrid iGPU-Only (disconnects dGPU on battery), Hybrid Auto, and UMA. Manage display refresh rate, resolution, display overdrive, and Auto Color Management.

Keyboard Lighting & Spectrum RGB

Manage Spectrum per-key RGB, 4-Zone RGB, rear port lighting, panel logo illumination, and white backlit keyboards without proprietary suites. Compatible with Windows Dynamic Lighting.

Actions, Macros & Smart Keys

Automate profile adjustments based on AC power state, battery percentage, lid position, display events, Wi-Fi connectivity, or game launch. Assign custom actions to Fn Smart Keys and display on-screen indicators.

Device Compatibility

Engineered for Lenovo gaming laptops from Generation 6 (2021) through Generation 11 (2026), with select feature support on Generation 5 (2020).

Legion Series

  • Legion 5, Legion 5 Pro, Legion Slim 5
  • Legion 7, Legion Pro 7, Legion Slim 7
  • Legion 9i flagship models
  • Legion Go handheld (8APU1)
  • Chinese models (R7000, R9000, Y7000, Y9000, G5000)

LOQ Series

  • Lenovo LOQ 15 (AMD and Intel)
  • Lenovo LOQ 16 (AMD and Intel)
  • LOQ Essential and Tower configurations
  • Power modes, battery charge controls, refresh rate switching, and backlighting

IdeaPad Gaming & Other Devices

  • IdeaPad Gaming 3 and 3 Pro
  • Lenovo Slim models
  • ThinkBook (supported via experimental ITS driver flag)
  • YOGA and XiaoXin variants (supported features vary by model)

Documentation & Reference

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical guidance grounded directly in the project codebase.

You can uninstall Vantage or use the built-in option in Lenovo Legion Toolkit Settings to disable Lenovo Vantage, Legion Zone, and Lenovo Hotkeys services. Disabling them in Settings halts their background processes and avoids hardware communication conflicts without removing the applications.

On clean Windows installations, ensure Lenovo Energy Management and Lenovo Vantage Gaming Feature Driver are installed so hardware interfaces are accessible. These drivers are not required on Generation 10 and newer hardware.

The standard Conservation Mode percentage is hardcoded in laptop firmware (EC) to 60% on 2021 and earlier models, or 80% on 2022 and later models. On select ThinkBook models supporting the PWRMGRV interface, custom charge start and stop thresholds can be configured.

Custom fan curves are supported in Custom Mode on Generation 7 (2022) and newer laptops whose firmware exposes fan table registers. Custom fan curves are not available in Quiet, Balance, or Performance modes, nor on Generation 6 and older models due to hardware limitations.

The application interacts with low-level Windows APIs (WMI, EC driver communication, keyboard hooks) which can trigger generic heuristic scanner alerts. All release installers are built publicly via GitHub Actions and verified with Authenticode signatures.
